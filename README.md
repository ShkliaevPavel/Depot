# 📦 Depot Service

<div id="header" align="left">

<h2>Depot Service — Accountability & Tracking</h2>

A data-driven depot management solution designed to improve **visibility, accountability, stock management, and supply planning** across clinical trial sites.

</div>

---

## 🎯 Key Values

* **Interactive reporting** — actively involves CRAs in the clinical trial supply process.
* **Data-driven resupply strategies** — helps prevent both overstocking and understocking.
* **Equipment tracking** — provides visibility of equipment status and supports calibration compliance.
* **Resupply forecasting** — helps ensure sites remain consistently and appropriately stocked.
* **Improved accountability** — strengthens the commitment of CRAs and sites to the trial team and study protocols.

---

## 📈 Industry Context

Data-driven supply chain management can generate significant operational improvements:

* **[Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/supply-chain/topics/future-of-supply-chain)** — reports that BI tools for supply chain management can achieve **up to 15% cost savings** and a **20–50% increase in efficiency**.

* **[McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/ai-driven-operations-forecasting-in-data-light-environments)** — reports that data analytics in supply chain operations can reduce costs by **up to 20%** and increase revenue by **10%**.

* **[International Journal of Supply Chain Management](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/386582685_The_Impact_of_Demand_Forecasting_Accuracy_on_Customer_Satisfaction)** — reports that improved forecasting accuracy was associated with customer satisfaction increasing to **97%**.

> **Note:** These figures represent findings reported by the respective sources and are provided as industry context rather than direct results of the particular clinical trial project.

# 💡 Solution

The solution consists of two interconnected reporting levels:

## 1. 🌍 Country-Specific Interactive Report

Designed for the **clinical trial team**, providing country-level visibility into depot and site supply activities.

### Key Features

* **Supply history & key metrics**
* **Shipment tracking**
* **Equipment & calibration tracking**
* **Resupply planner**
* **Country-level operational overview**

---

## 2. 🏢 General Study Report

Designed for the **Depot team** to provide a broader operational overview across the study.

### Includes

* All features available in the country-specific report
* **Extended statistics**
* **Depot balance**
* **Supply trends**
* **Purchase planning**
* **Resupply forecasting**
